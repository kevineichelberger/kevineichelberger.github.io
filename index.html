Lesson learned

Posting more won't fix posting the wrong thing.

The most common thing I hear: "We post every day and nothing happens." Usually the problem isn't frequency. It's that every post talks about the business instead of to the customer.

Quick test: read your last five captions. If most start with "We," rewrite one to start with "You" and watch the comments.
